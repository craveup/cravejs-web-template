# Community code of conduct

Crave.js should be a useful, rigorous and welcoming place for restaurant operators, designers,
developers and commerce specialists to collaborate.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Make room for different levels of technical, design and restaurant-operating experience.
- Protect private restaurant, customer, employee and security information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest, including vendor and sponsor relationships, when relevant.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, release evidence and community boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publication of private data,
credential sharing, deliberate disruption, spam and sustained bad-faith conduct are not accepted in
project spaces or when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [hello@craveup.com](mailto:hello@craveup.com) with the subject
`Crave.js conduct report`. Include relevant links and context, but do not forward credentials,
customer data or unrelated private conversations.

Crave maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, close discussions, reject work or
ban contributors when needed to protect people and the project. They will consider context, impact,
pattern, remediation and conflicts of interest. Public retaliation against a good-faith reporter is
itself unacceptable.
